Helmic Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

1. Overview

Helmic is an App Store Connect workspace for iPhone and Mac. It is strictly a client: you bring an API key that you created yourself in App Store Connect, and Helmic then talks directly to Apple from your device. We follow the principle of “data minimization.” There is no Helmic account to register, no server operated by us, and no analytics or advertising SDK built into the app. By default, all data stays on your device.

1.1 API Credentials You Supply (Keychain)

To sign a request, Helmic needs an App Store Connect API credential: the Issuer ID, the Key ID, the private key, and — for sales reports — your Vendor Number. These are saved to your device's Keychain under the service name com.helmic.app.appstore-connect and are used for one purpose only: to sign a short-lived token that is sent over HTTPS to api.appstoreconnect.apple.com.

  • We never receive your credential — we have no server to send it to
  • Private keys are never written to the app's runtime log
  • Removing the account inside Helmic deletes the Keychain item immediately; nothing else about it is retained

1.2 Your Store Data (Sales, Reviews, Versions, Builds)

When you open sales figures, customer reviews, versions or builds, Helmic requests that data from Apple on your behalf and displays it on screen. Because the request is signed with your own key, Apple returns only what your key is entitled to access. This data is held in memory for the current session and is cleared when the app quits; it is not uploaded to us and it is not shared with any third party.

1.3 Notifications and Background Refresh (Optional)

If you grant notification permission, Helmic posts local notifications on your device (for example, when a new App Store review arrives). Background refresh merely repeats the same read-only requests to Apple while the app is in the background. You can turn both off at any time in the app or in system settings.

1.4 App Analytics (Anonymous, Optional)

If you enable “Share Analytics with Developers” at the system level, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and performance data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any identity record — there is no sign-up
  • Payment card numbers or billing addresses — Helmic offers no in-app purchases and no subscriptions
  • Advertising identifiers and cross-app tracking data (none are used, so no tracking occurs)
  • The content of your store data — sales figures, reviews, app names and screenshots travel only between your device and Apple
  • We do not sell or share your data

3. Purpose of Data Usage

  • To authenticate your requests against the Apple App Store Connect API
  • To display and help you manage your own app's store data
  • To deliver the local notifications you explicitly enable
  • To improve stability using anonymous crash data

4. Third-Party Services Used

Service Purpose
Apple App Store Connect API The only network endpoint Helmic contacts, for reading and updating your own store data
Apple Keychain Storing the API credential you add, on-device
Apple Transporter (Mac, optional) Uploading a build file that you explicitly choose

No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK from any third party is embedded in the app.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • API credential: deleted as soon as you remove the account in Helmic's settings
  • Fetched store data: session memory only, gone when the app quits
  • Runtime log (at most the 200 most recent lines) and small display preferences: stored locally and clearable at any time
  • Sales reports are parsed in memory and are not written to disk

Because we operate no server, there is nothing on our side to export or erase: your data either lives on your device or is held by Apple under your own developer account.

6. Data Security

  • No Helmic-operated servers; all traffic goes directly over HTTPS/TLS to Apple
  • Credentials live in the system Keychain rather than in files or plain preferences
  • The Mac App Store build runs sandboxed with only two entitlements: outbound network access and read/write for files you explicitly select
  • Key and Issuer identifiers are masked in the interface by default and require an explicit action to reveal
  • Helmic relies on standard OS encryption only and is declared export-compliant as exempt encryption

7. Children’s Privacy

Helmic is a developer tool that requires an App Store Connect account. It is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect such information.
